# Before and during the event

Monitor current alerts from local authorities and weather services. Charge
communications equipment, prepare medicines and accessibility supplies, move
valuables only when it is safe, and keep the emergency kit ready to carry.
Leave early when ordered; an offline guide cannot overrule a live evacuation
instruction.

Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater. Depth, current, contamination,
road damage, and electrical hazards are difficult to judge. Keep away from
downed lines, unstable trees, damaged structures, fast water, and closed
roads.

# Afterward

Do not re-enter until authorities permit it. Treat wet electrical equipment,
fuel systems, contaminated food and water, mould, and structural damage as
specialist problems. Photograph damage only from a safe location and follow
current municipal, utility, insurer, and public-health instructions.

Related terms: flood, basement water, storm, tornado, blizzard, ice storm,
evacuation, weather alert, road closed, downed tree.
