# Leave when authorities order evacuation

Know more than one route out, keep a portable emergency kit ready, and follow
current local, Indigenous, provincial, or territorial authorities. When an
evacuation is ordered, leave promptly on official routes. Do not drive through
a wildfire or through an area with downed power lines. Do not return until
authorities say it is safe.

If a fire is approaching, report it to 9-1-1 or the local emergency number when
it is safe to call. Monitor current alerts and be ready to leave. Property
protection tasks must never delay evacuation.

Alert names and legal effects differ across Canada. An advisory, alert, order,
and rescission are not interchangeable, and this card cannot determine which
one applies at the operator's location. It also cannot know fire perimeters,
road closures, evacuation zones, shelter capacity, or whether any route is
currently safe.

This card is about leaving a wildfire area. Smoke-health guidance can recommend
remaining indoors and closing windows when no evacuation applies; current
official direction decides which action is appropriate.

Related terms: wildfire evacuation, evacuation order, evacuation alert,
wildland fire, leave now, official route, return home, fire perimeter.
