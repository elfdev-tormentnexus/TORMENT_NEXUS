# During a severe winter storm

Delay driving when conditions are dangerous and monitor current weather and
road information. If stranded in a vehicle, remaining inside is generally
safer than walking into a storm; keep the exhaust pipe clear of snow and use
fuel carefully while waiting for help.

# Prevent carbon-monoxide poisoning

- Never operate a generator, barbecue, camp stove, or other fuel-burning
  equipment inside a home, garage, basement, crawlspace, shed, or covered
  outdoor area.
- Place a portable generator at least 6 metres (20 feet) from buildings, with
  exhaust directed away from doors, windows, and vents.
- Never idle a vehicle in a garage, even with the garage door open.
- Keep appliance exhaust vents clear of snow and ice. Maintain
  battery-backed carbon-monoxide alarms near sleeping areas.

Prepare household and vehicle supplies before a storm, avoid prolonged
outdoor exertion, and check on people who may need help when conditions allow.
