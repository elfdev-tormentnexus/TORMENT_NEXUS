# Prevent exposure

Read the product label before every use and keep the product in its original,
labelled container. Follow ventilation, protective-equipment, storage, and
disposal directions. Keep chemicals secured away from children, animals,
food, heat, flame, and incompatible products.

Never mix household chemical products. In particular, bleach mixed with an
acid or ammonia can release dangerous gas. Do not improvise a neutralizer or
induce vomiting unless the product label or a qualified poison professional
specifically directs it.

## If exposure may have caused harm

Move away from ongoing fumes or danger without exposing another person. Follow
the first-aid directions on the exact product label or safety data sheet, and
contact a regional poison centre, health professional, or local emergency
service as appropriate. Keep the container or a clear photograph of its label
available for responders.

The correct response depends on the substance, amount, route, time, symptoms,
and person exposed. A generic offline card cannot safely replace the label or
current professional advice.
