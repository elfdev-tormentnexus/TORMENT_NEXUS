# Prepare before an emergency

Plan to manage without normal power, tap water, shops, or payment networks
for at least 72 hours. Keep supplies together, portable, easy to reach, and
known to everyone in the household. Review the plan and replace expired food,
water, batteries, and medicines regularly.

## Basic kit

- Store at least two litres of drinking water per person per day. Add more for
  food preparation, hygiene, pets, heat, illness, or accessibility needs.
- Keep shelf-stable food and a manual can opener.
- Include a battery or crank flashlight and radio, spare batteries, a first
  aid kit, small-denomination cash, extra keys, and a paper contact list.
- Add necessary medicines, copies of prescriptions, mobility or communication
  equipment, infant supplies, pet or service-animal supplies, warm clothing,
  and chargers or power banks appropriate to the household.
- Keep identification, essential documents, and the emergency plan protected
  from water.

## Make the plan usable

Choose meeting places and out-of-area contacts. Know how to leave the home by
more than one route. Decide who assists children, older adults, disabled
people, and animals. Follow evacuation orders and local official instructions;
a prewritten general plan cannot account for a live hazard.

Related terms: emergency kit, go bag, blackout supplies, disaster supplies,
