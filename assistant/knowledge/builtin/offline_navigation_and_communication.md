# Prepare before connectivity fails

Download maps for the required area while online and test that they open in
airplane mode. Keep a paper map, written destinations, important phone
numbers, and an out-of-area contact. Record where household emergency
supplies and meeting places are located; a device that knows the information
only while powered is not a complete plan.

Charge phones, radios, power banks, and spare batteries. Use each radio
according to its manual and the rules for the country, band, licence, and
channel. Meshtastic or LoRa can carry short messages between compatible,
configured nodes; it does not guarantee delivery, privacy, emergency-service
access, identity, location accuracy, or a populated mesh.

Send concise messages that state who, where, what is needed, and when the
information was current. Arrange check-in times so devices can remain off or
in low-power mode between them.

Related terms: offline map, GPS, no signal, LoRa, Meshtastic, T-Deck, radio,
power bank, paper map, emergency contact, communication plan.
