# During shaking

- Indoors: stay inside. Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Keep away from windows,
  doorways, elevators, and objects that can fall.
- Outdoors: remain outside and move to an open area away from buildings and
  power lines, then protect your head and neck.
- In a vehicle: pull over where it is safe, avoid bridges and overpasses, set
  the brake, and stay inside.

# Before and after

Secure tall or heavy furniture and appliances, keep heavy items low, practise
the response, and maintain household and vehicle emergency supplies.

After shaking, expect aftershocks. Check cautiously for fire, gas, electrical,
structural, spill, chimney, and downed-line hazards. Avoid unnecessary calls;
texting may leave voice lines available for emergencies. Leave a damaged
building when it is unsafe and follow current official instructions.

Coastal areas may also face tsunami risk. After strong or prolonged shaking,
follow the local tsunami plan and move using the current official route when
authorities or local natural-warning guidance calls for it. Exact risk zones,
routes, and earthquake early-warning coverage are live local facts.
